## What You’ll Need

Here’s a checklist of tools and resources to make your learning experience seamless and fun:

### Parental Permission and Guidance

Before starting this tutorial, we recommend that kids check with a parent or guardian for the following:
	1.	Internet Access:
This tutorial involves using online tools such as Google Earth, Teachable Machine, or Google Colab. Parental permission is advised to ensure appropriate use of these websites.
	2.	Account Creation:
Certain tools (e.g., Google Earth Engine or Kaggle) may require creating an account. Parents should assist in this process to meet age requirements and protect personal information.
	3.	Supervision:
While the tutorials are designed for young learners, some steps involve downloading software, using advanced tools, or handling datasets. Parental guidance can make the experience safer and more enjoyable.
