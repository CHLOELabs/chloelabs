# Bytelets

## Small guides for big curiosities

Bytelets are short, reusable project guides that help curious learners turn an
idea into something they can explore, make, test, or explain.

Each Bytelet will include:

- One clear question or challenge
- A short list of materials and tools
- An achievable sequence of steps
- Prompts for noticing, testing, and improving
- A reflection that can become part of a learner's lab notebook

## Designed to work with the Curiosity Engine

The Curiosity Engine helps a learner discover a direction. Bytelets will offer
extra structure when a project benefits from a reliable starting point.

They are guides, not answer sheets. Learners will still make the choices,
collect the evidence, create the work, and explain what they discovered.

:::info Bytelets are in development

The first collection is being designed and tested with Chloe, the first
ChloeLabs learner. In the meantime, start with a topic in
[The Curiosity Engine](/chloelabs/curiosity-engine).

:::

## For parents and learning partners

ChloeLabs is currently a prototype. An adult should review suggested projects,
third-party tools, account requirements, and safety considerations with younger
learners before they begin.
